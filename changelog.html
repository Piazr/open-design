Latest v0.2.0 June 5, 2026

Updated Content

Dynamic Workflows Trigger Word Ch 07, §7.1
The Dynamic Workflows trigger word changed from workflow to ultracode. The old trigger word caused false activations when "workflow" appeared in casual conversation. The new trigger word ultracode only fires when you explicitly want a dynamic workflow. The three usage options have been updated: (1) /deep-research, (2) explicit ultracode trigger word, (3) natural language description. All five prompt examples throughout the chapter updated from "Run a workflow" to "Use ultracode".
